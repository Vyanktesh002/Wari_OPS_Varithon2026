०१ The Problem

Six authorities. One Wari. Six separate truths.

A twenty-one day, two-hundred-and-fifty kilometre moving city. Half a million people on foot. Five hundred Dindis. Medical camps that appear and vanish overnight. And every authority keeping its own notebook, its own WhatsApp group, its own version of what is happening.

Dindi

Headcount and delay, known only to the Dindi.

Medical

A patient treated at Jejuri arrives at Wakhari with no history.

Police

A blocked route logged on paper, radioed to no one else.

Municipal

Water and shelter readiness, invisible until it fails.

Sanitation

Facility status held in a separate register entirely.

Supervisor

Asked to decide, with no way to see the whole.

No shared picture. Wari Command Intelligence is the connection layer.

०२ Five Movements
01

CONNECT

जोडणी

Six authorities that have never shared a screen now write into one operational state. Not a chat group — a structured, role-scoped record every other authority can read.

  • Role-based authentication
  • Scoped permissions per authority
  • One shared board, many views
02

OBSERVE

निरीक्षण

The Wari is more than a GPS dot. Halt readiness, camp load, ambulance count, medicine stock, shelter capacity, sanitation, weather — the whole moving environment, tracked.

  • Palkhi schedule vs actual
  • Dindi and crowd concentration
  • Twelve live operational overlays
03

DETECT

शोध

Meaningful change, not noise. The engine separates stable high pressure from pressure that is worsening fast, and suppresses the same alert repeating when nothing has actually moved.

  • Cross-domain correlation
  • Newly critical vs. steady state
  • Duplicate-alert suppression
04

PREDICT

अंदाज

Current state plus the last few ticks of history. A deterministic, explainable risk model — every score can be traced back to the readings that produced it.

  • Trend over recent history
  • Emerging risk, before it lands
  • Data freshness and confidence
05

PRIORITIZE

प्राधान्य

The supervisor should not have to discover the emerging situation by reading four hundred reports. The system says what deserves attention first, and why.

  • Ranked priority actions
  • Named resource gaps
  • End-of-day summary
